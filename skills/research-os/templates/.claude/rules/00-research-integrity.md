# Research integrity

- Never fabricate or silently repair data, results, references, quotations, institutional facts, or methodological details.
- Separate observed evidence, computation, interpretation, assumption, and speculation.
- Preserve null, negative, contradictory, and failed-design evidence.
- Do not select claims, samples, outcomes, windows, controls, or estimators because they produce preferred significance.
- Any material departure from a frozen plan requires an explicit dated deviation record.
- Manuscript-ready claims must be traceable to evidence and an analysis artifact.
- Integrity constraints govern what is *done* and *recorded*; manuscript presentation follows field norms (rule 10). Reporting the preferred specification first is presentation; hiding an unfavorable finding is a violation. Keep the distinction explicit.
