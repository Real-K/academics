# Wiki

- Search before creating; update rather than duplicate.
- Raw sources remain immutable; Wiki notes are structured derived memory.
- Every note has stable ID, type, status, date, provenance, and typed links.
- Mark evidence level and uncertainty. Contradictions are first-class notes.
- Keep `Wiki/index.md` and `Wiki/log.md` current and run the linter after material changes.
