# Finance theory and contribution

- Explain economic actors, incentives, information, constraints, mechanism, response, and equilibrium or behavioral implication.
- Verify that the institutional/legal channel exists in the sample period.
- Frame a contribution against the closest papers, not a broad topic.
- Prefer hypotheses that discriminate among mechanisms and state boundary conditions.
- Do not infer a mechanism solely from a coefficient sign.
