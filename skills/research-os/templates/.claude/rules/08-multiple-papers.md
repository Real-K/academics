# Multiple-paper portfolio

- Each paper has an independent folder, research charter, state, decisions, design, code, analysis, manuscript, and submission record.
- Shared data and methods are reused through registries, not copied and silently modified.
- A finding may inform another paper only through a documented shared asset or Wiki link.
- Do not let one paper's preferred mechanism or result contaminate another paper's design.
- Update `paper_registry.csv` and `PORTFOLIO_STATE.md` when status or priority changes.
