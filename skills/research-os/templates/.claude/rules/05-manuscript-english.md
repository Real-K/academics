# Manuscript English

- Draft the paper in publication-quality English.
- One paragraph, one argumentative function. Lead with the claim, then evidence and implication.
- Use causal verbs only when the frozen identification design supports them.
- Keep all reported numbers synchronized with machine-generated artifacts.
- Avoid inflated novelty, generic transitions, vague antecedents, and literature laundry lists.
- Record every substantive claim in `CLAIMS_LEDGER.csv`.
- Follow finance presentation norms (rule 10): final logical structure, no design-chronology narration, no preregistration vocabulary, alternatives reported as robustness/appendix.
