# Continuity and stage gates

- Start by reading active-state and handoff records; do not restart work from conversation memory.
- End material work by updating current state, decision log, session handoff, and next action.
- Stage transitions require explicit gate evidence in `docs/WORKFLOW.md`.
- After pre-analysis freeze, protect design files and record every deviation before change.
- Frozen main results are append-only. New analyses go to new versioned run folders.
- Do not repeat a completed task unless validation failed or new evidence justifies reopening it.
- Freezes, gates, and deviation records are *internal audit boundaries*. They bind analysis conduct and the project record; they are not manuscript content and impose no obligation to narrate design chronology in papers (rule 10).
